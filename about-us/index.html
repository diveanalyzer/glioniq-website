About Us — Our Story

A Healthy Body. A Clear Mind. A Fuller Life.

Every day, millions of people invest in their physical health.

They join gyms, pay monthly memberships, follow exercise programs, and dedicate time, money, and effort to maintaining their physical strength and well-being.

They understand that taking care of their bodies today may help them enjoy healthier, more active lives tomorrow.

But what about the brain?

We work to keep our bodies strong as we age. Yet how often do we give the same attention to maintaining our cognitive abilities?

What is the value of reaching an advanced age with a healthy body if we can no longer think clearly, remember meaningful moments, communicate with loved ones, or fully experience the world around us?

Living longer is important.

But preserving our cognitive abilities, independence, and quality of life as we age is just as important.

At GlionIQ, we believe that caring for the brain deserves the same attention, commitment, and long-term perspective as caring for the body.

The Importance of Cognitive Reserve

One of the most fascinating concepts in modern neuroscience is cognitive reserve.

Cognitive reserve refers to the brain’s ability to maintain cognitive functioning and adapt in the face of aging-related changes or neurological challenges.

Research suggests that lifelong learning, intellectually stimulating activities, education, social engagement, and other enriching experiences may contribute to cognitive reserve.

A greater cognitive reserve may help some individuals maintain cognitive functioning for longer, even when age-related or disease-related changes occur in the brain.

This is an important scientific idea behind GlionIQ.

We believe that curiosity, learning, and meaningful cognitive engagement should remain part of life at every age.

The goal is not simply to add more years to life, but to support the quality of those years.

Science, Responsibility, and Realistic Expectations

We believe that scientific integrity begins with understanding both the potential and the limitations of research.

Regular physical exercise, balanced nutrition, and healthy habits can support physical health and may reduce certain health risks. However, they cannot guarantee that a person will never develop a disease.

The same principle applies to brain health.

Learning, cognitive stimulation, and intellectually engaging activities may contribute to cognitive well-being, but they cannot guarantee the prevention of dementia, Alzheimer’s disease, cognitive decline, or other neurological conditions.

Human health is influenced by many interconnected factors, including genetics, environmental exposures, cardiovascular health, lifestyle, medical conditions, and biological processes that are not fully within our control.

There is no absolute protection and no 100% guarantee.

Research suggests that addressing modifiable risk factors and maintaining a healthy, intellectually engaging lifestyle may contribute to healthier aging and, in some circumstances, help reduce the risk of cognitive decline.

However, the benefits of any specific cognitive training application must be evaluated through appropriate scientific evidence.

Our mission is not to promise the impossible. Our mission is to turn scientific knowledge into meaningful opportunities for learning, cognitive engagement, and lifelong development.

The Science Behind Our Name

Every great idea begins with a connection.

The name GlionIQ was born from the fascinating world of neuroscience and the remarkable way our brains learn, adapt, and develop throughout life.

At the heart of our name are three concepts:

GLIA — The Support Behind Intelligence

Glial cells are essential components of the nervous system.

Far from being merely supporting structures, they actively contribute to maintaining healthy neural environments, regulating communication, and supporting processes involved in learning and memory.

NEURON — The Power of Connection

Neurons are the brain’s information-processing cells.

Through intricate networks of electrical and chemical signals, they enable us to think, learn, remember, and interact with the world.

IQ — The Pursuit of Knowledge

IQ represents our commitment to intelligence, curiosity, learning, and the lifelong pursuit of knowledge.

Where Science Meets Purpose

The interaction between neurons and glial cells reflects an extraordinary principle of the human brain: its abilities emerge not from individual cells working alone, but from complex systems of communication and cooperation.

This principle inspired GlionIQ.

We believe that learning should never stop, that curiosity belongs to every generation, and that engaging the mind can enrich life at every age.

From early childhood development to lifelong learning and cognitive engagement in later adulthood, our vision is to create meaningful digital experiences that encourage discovery, knowledge, and intellectual growth.

Our Scientific Commitment

GlionIQ is committed to developing educational and cognitive engagement experiences informed by scientific research.

Our work draws inspiration from neuroscience, developmental psychology, cognitive science, and research into cognitive reserve and healthy aging.

We aim to continuously review relevant scientific developments and improve our products as new evidence becomes available.
