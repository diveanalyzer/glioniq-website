Custom Breathing

The future application will allow users to customize breathing durations and select suitable patterns. Custom Breathing is a configuration feature, not a fourth primary method.

Science and responsible expectations

Breathing patterns can provide a structure for mindful practice. These exact timing patterns are not presented as individually proven medical treatments. No guaranteed treatment of insomnia, anxiety or neurological conditions is claimed.

Breathe comfortably

  • Breathing exercises are wellness activities, not medical treatments.
  • Breathe comfortably without forcing breath holds.
  • Stop if dizziness or discomfort occurs.
