Children’s Learning & Development Laboratories

Trampolina

A growing world of discovery for children, bringing learning, exploration and creative thinking into interactive digital experiences.

Intended audience
Children and the families and educators who support their learning. Activities are intended to accommodate different interests and stages of development, without a fixed age range.
Purpose
To create opportunities to explore foundational ideas, try new approaches and enjoy the process of learning.
Development status
In development. Activity availability and release details will be confirmed before public access is offered.

Learning with curiosity

Trampolina’s educational direction emphasizes exploration and opportunities to practise. Digital activities are intended to complement the wider experiences through which children learn.

Availability

Confirmed feature details will be added as activities are ready to share. No public download or store release is announced here.
