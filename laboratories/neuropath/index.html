Cognitive Engagement Laboratories

NeuroPath

A suite of brain-training labs for memory, attention, logic and cognitive flexibility.

Intended audience
People interested in ongoing cognitive engagement and lifelong learning, including adults exploring structured challenges at their own pace.
Purpose
To provide a coherent collection of digital activities organized around different kinds of cognitive engagement.
Development status
In development. This website introduces the laboratories and their confirmed activity organization; it does not provide the mobile activities or announce a public release.

Four connected laboratories

Explore Memory Lab, Attention Lab, Logic Lab and Flexibility Lab. Each has its own focus within the wider NeuroPath platform.

Confirmed activity organization

  • Memory Lab contains the signature activity Memory Lab.
  • Logic Lab contains NeuroWeave and CodeQuest.

Light Paths

Light Paths is an existing NeuroPath activity. Its laboratory category is awaiting confirmation, so it is not assigned to a laboratory here.

Engagement with realistic expectations

NeuroPath is not a medical treatment. No activity is presented as clinically proven to prevent dementia, Alzheimer’s disease or cognitive decline, or to build cognitive reserve. Product-specific benefits require appropriate evidence.
