The science behind our work

Science & Research

Science-informed technology begins with curiosity, careful questions and respect for evidence.

Intended audience
For anyone interested in the research principles that inform GlionIQ’s work.
Purpose
To explain our approach to responsible educational and cognitive-engagement experiences.
Development status
Our research overview will evolve as approved reviews and product-specific evidence become available.

Research-inspired, evidence-aware

GlionIQ aims to draw on neuroscience, developmental psychology, educational research and the study of lifelong learning. These fields provide questions and perspectives for product development; inspiration alone is not proof of a product’s effectiveness.

Cognitive reserve and healthy aging

Cognitive reserve, cognitive engagement and healthy aging are part of the scientific context explored in our company story. We distinguish broad research ideas from evidence about a particular digital activity.

How we approach evidence

  • Review relevant research and its limitations.
  • Distinguish hypotheses and design intentions from demonstrated outcomes.
  • Evaluate claims in the context of the population and methods studied.
  • Improve products when appropriate evidence supports a change.

Clear limits

Our digital laboratories are not certified clinical laboratories or medical treatments. We do not guarantee prevention of Alzheimer’s disease, dementia or cognitive decline. Research references will accompany future research articles.
